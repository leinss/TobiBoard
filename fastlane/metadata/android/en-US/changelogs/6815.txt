- Fixed the keyboard crashing when a voice transcription failed, which took your recording with it.
- A failed transcription now keeps the recording and offers Retry, so a long dictation is no longer lost. The clip is deleted as soon as a retry succeeds, and expires on its own after two minutes.
- The recording timer now shows the time limit alongside the elapsed time, and warns in the last ten seconds.
- New optional setting: also copy each finished transcription to the clipboard, as a safety net for long dictations.
